# Daily stand-up (asynchronous)

Because our timetables differ, the daily Scrum is held asynchronously in the group chat.

Every weekday by 9 pm each member posts:

- **Done:** what I finished since my last update
- **Next:** what I will do next
- **Blocked:** anything stopping me (or "nothing")

The Scrum Master reads the updates, helps remove blockers and updates Jira. Screenshots of the stand-up messages are kept as evidence.
